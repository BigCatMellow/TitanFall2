# Explicit traversal-action prototype

Separate canonical-repository worktree/branch; never mixed into the frozen v24
study or normal installation. Native0BB7182E... is a default-off prototype.
It owns known type16 pilot pursuit jumps through alignment, first press,
airborne release, one second press and grounded landing verification. It is
interruptible by combat/new goals/death and bounded by time/progress checks.
Failed actions hand back to the existing local detour; no teleportation,
velocity boost, aim/warm-up, weapon or human ability changes.

All19Rust policy suites (103tests) and pinned offline release-debug build pass.
Original dependency graph/Windows Cargo.lock SHA E4F84348... retained. Four
new tests cover two released presses, landing, geometry/type gating, clock,
progress, lost-entry and failure bounds. The live isolated Eden/War Games test
is retained separately. Initial live evidence includes successful grounded
arrival on War Games and many EarlyLanding failures. It is not ready for normal
play. A leading next hypothesis is approach/run-up momentum: stopping to align
can leave too little horizontal speed for long crossings. Further collision
feasibility and typed-action coverage also remain open.

No claim that virtual inputs are successful casts, that all links are traversable,
or that this controller improves human difficulty. Raw logs/binaries stay local.
